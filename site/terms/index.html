1. Agreement

These Terms & Conditions apply to every service provided by Pristine Steam Cleaning ("we", "us") to you, the customer. By booking a service, approving a quote, or letting us begin work, you agree to these terms and to our Service Agreement and Privacy Policy.

2. Our services

We provide residential carpet cleaning, upholstery cleaning, and tile and grout cleaning in Houston and nearby cities. We use hot water extraction and professional cleaning solutions. We may decline any job that we believe could damage the material or is unsafe to perform.

3. Quotes and pricing

Prices on our website and estimates given by phone, text or online are based on the information you give us. Your final price is confirmed during a walkthrough at your home before any cleaning starts. If the job is larger or different from what was described, we will tell you the new price and you can approve it, change the scope, or cancel without charge at that point.

Visits have a $150 minimum unless a promotion such as the 3 Room Special says otherwise. Promotions cannot be combined unless we say so in writing.

4. Booking, rescheduling and cancellations

We give you an arrival window when you book. Please reschedule or cancel as early as you can so we can offer the time to another customer.

  • More than 48 hours' notice: no fee.
  • 24 to 48 hours' notice: $40 fee.
  • Less than 24 hours' notice, or no one home at the scheduled time: $75 fee.

We may waive fees for emergencies or severe weather. If we need to reschedule, we will contact you as soon as possible and there is no charge to you.

5. Your responsibilities

  • An adult (18+) must be present to let us in, complete the walkthrough and approve the price.
  • Provide access to working water and electricity.
  • Move small, fragile and valuable items, and anything on the floor, before we arrive.
  • Keep children and pets away from the work area while we clean and until the floors are dry.
  • Tell us about any known damage, loose seams, recent repairs, or delicate fabrics before we start.

6. Results and limitations

We work hard to remove every stain we can, but some cannot be removed. This includes bleach spots, dye transfers, burns, rust, permanent pet urine damage, sun fading and wear. We will point out anything we expect not to come out during the walkthrough.

We are not responsible for pre-existing damage, color loss or shrinkage caused by a manufacturer defect or an earlier cleaning, seams or carpet that were not installed correctly, or fabrics that are not labeled as safe for wet cleaning.

7. Payment

Payment is due when the work is finished, unless we have agreed otherwise in writing. We accept major cards, including Tap to Pay, through our booking system. Invoices not paid within 7 days may be charged a late fee of 10% of the unpaid balance.

8. If something is not right

If you are not happy with an area we cleaned, contact us within 72 hours of the service. We will come back and re-clean that area at no charge. Re-cleaning is our first remedy and must be requested before any refund is considered.

9. Damage and liability

We take care in every home. If you believe we caused damage, tell us within 72 hours and give us a chance to inspect it. Our total liability for any claim is limited to the amount you paid for the service, except where Texas law does not allow that limit. We are not liable for indirect or consequential damages.

10. Photos

We may take before and after photos of the areas we clean to document the job. We will not post photos online that show your home's address, your family or personal items without your permission.

11. Governing law

These terms are governed by the laws of the State of Texas. Any dispute will be handled in the county in Texas where the service was performed.
