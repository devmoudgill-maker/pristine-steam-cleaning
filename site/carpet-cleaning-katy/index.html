Our 4-step deep clean

The same process in every room, every time. It's why traffic lanes disappear instead of coming back next week.

  1. 1

    Inspect & pre-treat

    We walk the rooms with you, flag tough spots, and apply a pre-treatment matched to the stains and traffic lanes.

  2. 2

    Agitate

    A grooming brush works the solution into the base of the fibers to loosen soil, oils and pet hair.

  3. 3

    Hot water extraction

    Hot water rinses the carpet and powerful suction pulls it right back out, along with the dirt.

  4. 4

    Groom & speed dry

    We set the pile, protect furniture legs, and get air moving so your floors dry faster.
