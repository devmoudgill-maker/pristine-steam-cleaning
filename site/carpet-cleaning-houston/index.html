Deep cleaning for Houston homes

Houston is hard on carpet. Our humidity keeps fibers damp longer, summer storms track mud straight in from the yard, and a lot of homes here are a mix of old carpet, newer tile and rugs that have been through a few moves. Surface vacuuming can't reach what settles at the base of the fibers, so traffic lanes go gray and spots come back after a week.

We pre-treat every room, work the solution in, then rinse and extract with hot water so the soil leaves with the water instead of sitting in the pad. Whether it's a one-bedroom in Midtown or a two-story in Spring Branch, you get the same deep clean and a clear price before we start.

Our 4-step deep clean

The same process in every room, every time. It's why traffic lanes disappear instead of coming back next week.

  1. 1

    Inspect & pre-treat

    We walk the rooms with you, flag tough spots, and apply a pre-treatment matched to the stains and traffic lanes.

  2. 2

    Brush it loose

    A grooming brush works the solution into the base of the fibers to loosen soil, oils and pet hair.

  3. 3

    Hot water extraction

    Hot water rinses the carpet and powerful suction pulls it right back out, along with the dirt.

  4. 4

    Groom & speed dry

    We set the pile, protect furniture legs, and get air moving so your floors dry faster.

Houston carpet cleaning FAQ

Don't see yours? Call or text (346) 666-6223.

How long does carpet take to dry in Houston?

Most carpets are dry to the touch in a few hours. On very humid days full drying can take longer, so we pull out as much water as we can and recommend running your AC and ceiling fans for the rest of the day.

Do you clean apartments, condos and townhomes?

Yes. We clean homes of every size across Houston. Let us know about parking, gate codes or elevator rules when you book so the visit goes smoothly.

Can you get out old pet stains?

Fresh and recent pet accidents usually come out well with pre-treatment and extraction. Older urine that has soaked into the pad can leave permanent marks or odor, and we'll be upfront about that during the walkthrough.
