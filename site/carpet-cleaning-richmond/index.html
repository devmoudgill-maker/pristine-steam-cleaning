Deep cleaning for Richmond homes

New construction carpet often comes with a surprise: builder dust and drywall powder that settles into the fibers during the build. After the first year, add pet hair, lawn dirt and the usual spills, and the carpet starts to feel flat and look dull.

We pre-treat, agitate and rinse with hot water extraction so the carpet fibers stand back up and the dirt comes out with the water. It's a good way to reset a home after moving in, after a renovation, or before family comes in for the holidays.

Our 4-step deep clean

The same process in every room, every time. It's why traffic lanes disappear instead of coming back next week.

  1. 1

    Inspect & pre-treat

    We walk the rooms with you, flag tough spots, and apply a pre-treatment matched to the stains and traffic lanes.

  2. 2

    Agitate

    A grooming brush works the solution into the base of the fibers to loosen soil, oils and pet hair.

  3. 3

    Hot water extraction

    Hot water rinses the carpet and powerful suction pulls it right back out, along with the dirt.

  4. 4

    Groom & speed dry

    We set the pile, protect furniture legs, and get air moving so your floors dry faster.

Richmond carpet cleaning FAQ

Don't see yours? Call or text (346) 666-6223.

Should I clean brand-new carpet?

A light clean in the first year removes construction dust and keeps the carpet looking new longer. Most homeowners book once or twice a year after that.

Do you clean area rugs?

We clean area rugs on-site when the material allows it. Send us a photo with your quote request and we'll confirm.

Do I need to move furniture?

Please move small items like toys, lamps and anything breakable. We can work around larger furniture, and we'll talk about it during the walkthrough.
