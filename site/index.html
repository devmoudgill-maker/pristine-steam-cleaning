Our 4-step deep clean

The same process in every room, every time. It's why traffic lanes disappear instead of coming back next week.

  1. 1

    Inspect & pre-treat

    We walk the rooms with you, flag tough spots, and apply a pre-treatment matched to the stains and traffic lanes.

  2. 2

    Brush it loose

    A grooming brush works the solution into the base of the fibers to loosen soil, oils and pet hair.

  3. 3

    Hot water extraction

    Hot water rinses the carpet and powerful suction pulls it right back out, along with the dirt.

  4. 4

    Groom & speed dry

    We set the pile, protect furniture legs, and get air moving so your floors dry faster.

A deeper clean for a healthier home

Carpet works like a filter. It holds on to what floats through your house until something pulls it out.

Dust, pollen and allergens
Houston's long pollen seasons send it indoors on shoes and clothes. Hot water extraction rinses it out of the fibers instead of stirring it up.
Pets and little ones
Pet dander, accidents and dropped snacks settle where kids and dogs spend the most time. We pre-treat those spots and rinse them clean.
Humidity and odors
Damp air keeps smells hanging around. Removing the soil that holds them helps your rooms smell fresh again.

How much does carpet cleaning cost in Houston?

Our 3 Room Special is $98 for three carpeted rooms, walk-in closets included. Additional rooms are $45 each, sofas are $99, chairs are $45, and tile and grout is $0.75 per square foot. Visits have a $150 minimum unless you book the special.

What cleaning method do you use?

Hot water extraction, often called steam cleaning. We pre-treat, brush the solution in, then rinse with hot water and extract it so the dirt leaves with the water.

How long until I can walk on my carpet?

Most carpet is dry to the touch in a few hours. You can walk on it in clean socks right away. Running the AC and ceiling fans helps, especially on humid Houston days.

Do I need to move my furniture?

Please move small and fragile items. We can work around larger furniture and will talk through it during the walkthrough.

Will every stain come out?

Most do. Some, like bleach spots, dye, burns and old pet urine that soaked into the pad, can be permanent. We'll tell you what to expect before we start.
