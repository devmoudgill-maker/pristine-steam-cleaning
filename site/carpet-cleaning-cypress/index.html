Deep cleaning for Cypress homes

Spring in Cypress means a yellow film of pollen on cars, patios and, sooner or later, your carpet. It comes in on shoes, paws and clothes and settles into the fibers where vacuums only reach the top.

Hot water extraction rinses the carpet from the base up. We pre-treat, agitate, rinse and extract, then groom the fibers so they dry standing up. It's a good habit at the start of spring, after summer, and before the holidays.

Our 4-step deep clean

The same process in every room, every time. It's why traffic lanes disappear instead of coming back next week.

  1. 1

    Inspect & pre-treat

    We walk the rooms with you, flag tough spots, and apply a pre-treatment matched to the stains and traffic lanes.

  2. 2

    Brush it loose

    A grooming brush works the solution into the base of the fibers to loosen soil, oils and pet hair.

  3. 3

    Hot water extraction

    Hot water rinses the carpet and powerful suction pulls it right back out, along with the dirt.

  4. 4

    Groom & speed dry

    We set the pile, protect furniture legs, and get air moving so your floors dry faster.

Cypress carpet cleaning FAQ

Don't see yours? Call or text (346) 666-6223.

Does carpet cleaning help with allergies?

Deep cleaning removes a lot of the dust, pollen and pet dander that build up in carpet fibers. Many families notice their home feels fresher after a clean, especially in spring.

Can you clean the same day?

Sometimes. Call us and we'll check the schedule. Pristine Club members get priority and same-day booking when spots are open.

Do you use harsh chemicals?

We use professional carpet cleaning solutions and rinse them out with hot water so residue doesn't stay in the carpet. Keep kids and pets off the floor until it's dry.
