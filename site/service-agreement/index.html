Your Pristine Experience & Service Agreement

Effective October 15, 2026

1. Before we arrive

  • You will receive a confirmation with your date and arrival window.
  • Vacuum high-traffic areas if you can and pick up small items, toys and anything breakable.
  • Plan where pets will stay during the cleaning.
  • Let us know about parking, gate codes or building rules ahead of time.

See our full prep checklist.

2. The walkthrough

When we arrive, we walk through each area with you, look at stains and traffic lanes, and point out anything that may not come out. We confirm your final price before we begin. Nothing is cleaned until you approve it.

3. The clean

Every clean follows the same four steps: inspect and pre-treat, agitate, hot water extraction, then groom and speed dry. Hard furniture may be protected with tabs or blocks under the legs; please leave them in place until the carpet is fully dry.

4. After we leave

  • Most carpet is dry to the touch within a few hours. Full drying can take longer on humid days.
  • Run your AC and ceiling fans to speed up drying.
  • Walk on damp carpet in clean socks only, and take care on hard floors next to wet carpet since they can be slippery.
  • Keep pets and kids off the carpet until it is dry.
  • If a spot reappears after drying ("wicking"), contact us within 72 hours and we will come back to treat it.

5. Pristine Club membership

The Pristine Club is a monthly membership for $39 per month. Membership includes:

  • Four seasonal deep cleanings per year, each covering up to 3 carpeted rooms.
  • 50% off additional services booked on your member cleanings.
  • Priority booking, and same-day booking when spots are available.
  • Refer a friend: when someone you refer books and pays for a service, your next cleaning is free.
  • Member-only offers we share throughout the year.

Membership is billed monthly to the card on file. Seasonal cleanings are scheduled about every three months and do not roll over if skipped. You can cancel at any time by contacting us; cancellation takes effect at the end of the current billing month. Our Terms & Conditions apply to every member cleaning.

6. Our promise

We will show up on time, treat your home with care, give you an honest price before we begin, and make it right if something isn't right.
